# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Software engineers and curious builders looking for practical, in-depth notes on backend engineering, cloud-native systems, security, and modern programming.

## Product Purpose

Immersed in Code is a personal technical blog and portfolio. It turns hands-on engineering experience into readable tutorials, field notes, and a visible record of the author's journey. Success means readers can quickly find useful writing and understand the author's technical practice.

## Positioning

The site combines technical publishing with a living engineering timeline and a small portfolio of shipped tools.

## Operating Context

Readers arrive from search, social links, or the home page, scan recent posts and topics, then read a full article. The home page also communicates the author's experience and projects.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static Docusaurus 3 site using TypeScript, React, Markdown/MDX, and a custom blog plugin.
- Blog posts, tag navigation, article search, timeline expansion, dark mode, and external project/social links must remain functional.
- Content and factual claims come from the existing repository; the redesign must not invent testimonials, metrics, or projects.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Immersed in Code
- Existing tagline: “Simplicity is the soul of efficiency.”
- Existing logo and blog imagery remain available.

## Evidence on Hand

- Blog posts and article imagery in `blog/`.
- Portfolio and career data in `src/data/timeline.ts`.
- Brand assets in `static/img/`.

## Product Principles

- Make technical writing easy to discover.
- Let the author's point of view lead before interface decoration.
- Make depth and chronology feel connected.
- Keep interactions light, clear, and useful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic links/headings, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
